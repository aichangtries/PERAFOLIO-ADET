Reflection Journal
Week of: September 21, 2026

My goal this week
My goal this week is to stop treating PeraFolio as just a design and start turning it into the actual Flutter project. I want to get the foundation working first, especially the reusable components, navigation, and main screens, instead of changing the design again every time I notice something I could improve.

What I did
I used my revised proposal, mockup, and design system as the basis for the final project instead of starting from scratch again. I kept the main PeraFolio flow focused on Dashboard, Activity, Accounts, Transfer, and Pay, with onboarding and supporting profile/settings screens around them. I also carried over the reusable components I identified earlier so I do not have to style every screen separately.

I started documenting the project at the same time instead of leaving the README and reports until the end. I also started tracking how I use AI. I use it to explain code, help troubleshoot problems, suggest structures, and organize documentation, but I still need to understand the code I keep and be able to explain the parts I write myself.


What blocked me
Layout is still one of the areas that slows me down. I usually know how I want a screen to look, but spacing and placement can take a lot of trial and error once I build it in Flutter. Overflow is also something I need to watch because a layout that looks fine in one case may break when the available space changes.

Another gap is persistence. I already decided to use Hive CE for local structured storage, but choosing it in the proposal is different from actually implementing it. I still need to test saving and restoring a small amount of data before connecting persistence to the whole app.

What I learned
One thing I am carrying over from Modules 4 and 5 is that making something work and making it look finished are two different parts of the work. Even when the logic is simple, spacing, validation, navigation, and different UI states can take a lot of time.

I also learned that I need to document changes while I make them. During M7A1 and M7A3, I kept changing parts of PeraFolio even after I thought I was finished, and it became difficult to remember every revision afterward. For finals, I want my commits, report, journal, README, and AI usage record to grow with the project instead of being reconstructed at the end.
